01 / What is Portus?

A city-building game with a memory.

Portus is a slow strategy game where survival is shaped by resources, warnings, favour, research, and story. You build, endure, and read the world as it changes around you.

The game is part settlement sim, part mythic archive. Its systems are practical, but its atmosphere belongs to dreams, ruins, coastlines, and old powers moving under ordinary decisions.
