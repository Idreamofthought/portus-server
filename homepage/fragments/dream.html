Dream Fragment

A drifting moment from the dream-realm.

At the edge of the town, the street forgot where it was going. It softened into water, then into sky, and every window held the same pale moon.

I walked until the houses began to breathe. Somewhere behind them, a bell rang once. The sound did not travel through the air; it passed through the ground, waking small lights beneath the stones.

When I turned back, the road had remembered me.
