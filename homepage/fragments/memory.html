Memory Vignette

A small recollection, held gently.

My grandmother kept buttons in a blue glass jar. They clicked softly when she searched for one, as though a little weather lived among them.

On winter afternoons, I sat beneath the kitchen table and watched her hands sort the colours. She never threw any away. A button without a coat was still waiting for its story.

That is how I learned that keeping something is not the same as knowing where it belongs.
