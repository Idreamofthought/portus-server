Ecological Note

A quiet observation of the living world.

After the rain, the pavement kept a second sky. Small pools gathered between the paving stones, each one holding a trembling reflection of a cloud.

Moss had found the north side of the old wall. It did not hurry. It only continued, green upon grey, turning neglect into a kind of shelter.

By evening, the sparrows had emptied the silence.
