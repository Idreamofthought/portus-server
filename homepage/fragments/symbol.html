Symbolic Note

A reflection on a symbol or image.

A door is never only a door. It is the shape a question takes when it stands upright.

We place them in walls to divide one room from another, then spend our lives listening for what moves on the other side. Some doors open outward. Some open inward. A few remain closed until we become someone who can enter.

The handle is the smallest promise: there is a way through.
