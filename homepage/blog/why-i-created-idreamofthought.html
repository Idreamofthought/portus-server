Site identity

Why
this place?

I Dream of Thought is a home for writing that did not want to become only a feed, a portfolio, or a folder.

Essay

A creative archive with doors.

The site gives poems, essays, fragments, worldbuilding, and games one shared structure. A reader can enter through a branch, a leaf, a blog post, or the Portus harbour.

That structure matters because the work is relational. A fragment can become a system. A poem can become lore. A game can send someone back toward an essay with new eyes.
