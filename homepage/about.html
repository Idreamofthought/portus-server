About

I Dream of Thought is a small port on the edge of the Mediterranean — a place where ideas arrive like boats and leave like stories. It is a space for fragments: dreams, symbols, memory, and the quiet ecology of imagination.

This site gathers reflections, small texts, images, and the world of Portus, a contemplative strategy game about time, fate, and the fragile balance of places.

Philosophy

The project is built on a simple belief: that thought is not a straight line, but a tide. It rises, recedes, returns, and carries with it traces of what we have seen, felt, and forgotten.

Here, nothing is rushed. The pace is slow, the tone is gentle, and the world grows in layers — like sediment, like memory, like dusk settling over the sea.

Portus

Portus is the heart of this place: a game about building, surviving, interpreting signs, and navigating the favour of time, chaos, destiny, and twilight. It is both playful and reflective — a quiet strategy game shaped by myth, ecology, and the rhythms of Mediterranean life.

You can learn more on the Portus page.

Creator

This project is created and maintained with care, curiosity, and a love for atmospheric worlds. It is an ongoing work — a living space that will grow with new fragments, new ideas, and new stories.
