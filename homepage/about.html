About
the project.

I Dream of Thought is an ongoing creative space for writing, thinking, and making atmospheric worlds.

01 / The philosophy

Thought is a tide.

It rises, recedes, returns, and carries traces of what we have seen, felt, and forgotten. This project gives those traces somewhere to settle.

The work moves slowly on purpose. It is interested in attention, ambiguity, ecology, memory, and the strange meanings that gather between ordinary things.

02 / The making

Writing is the trunk.

Poems, prose, fragments, and essays are the centre of the site. Portus grows from the same soil: a world where systems can feel like moods, and a landscape can remember.

The tree is deliberately unfinished. New branches appear when a question refuses to disappear.
