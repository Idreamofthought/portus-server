About Portus

Idreamofthought is a concept — a space for writing, thinking, and dreaming. It holds fragments, reflections, symbolic notes, ecological observations, and memory vignettes. It is the quiet, creative side of the project.

Portus is the game. A work in progress, a dream-realm where you gather resources, build what you need to survive, and slowly shape a town from the mist.

Philosophy

The project is built on a simple belief: that thought is not a straight line, but a tide. It rises, recedes, returns, and carries with it traces of what we have seen, felt, and forgotten.

Here, nothing is rushed. The pace is slow, the tone is gentle, and the world grows in layers — like sediment, like memory, like dusk settling over the sea.

Portus

You begin with almost nothing. You gather wood, stone, food, and meaning. You place buildings, unlock new possibilities, and try to keep your settlement alive as the world shifts around you.

Portus is still growing — new systems, new buildings, new stories — but the heart of it is simple: survive, build, and watch your town take shape in the dream.

You can learn more on the Portus page.

Creator

This project is created and maintained with care, curiosity, and a love for atmospheric worlds. It is an ongoing work — a living space that will grow with new fragments, new ideas, and new stories.
