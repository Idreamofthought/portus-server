Lore / Warnings

The world
speaks first.

Warnings make Portus feel alive: not random, but attentive, readable, and uneasy.

Signs

A warning is a threshold.

Warnings can rise from hunger, resource strain, unstable weather, spiritual imbalance, or patterns too old for the settlement to understand.

The player should be able to act on a warning without fully mastering its meaning. That gap is where Portus becomes mythic.
