Disaster
leaves lessons.

In Portus, catastrophe is not only punishment. It is a change in the world that must be read, repaired, and remembered.

Meaning

Ruin is part of the archive.

Flood, fire, shortage, storm, and stranger disturbances create pressure on the settlement. The best warnings let a player prepare; the best disasters leave a story behind.

Each disaster should create a practical problem, a visible mark, and a route toward recovery.
