Professional Services Terms (B2B)

These terms apply to professional services provided under CAST and RMJ English to business clients.

1. Scope

Services may include strategic communication support, English editing and proofreading, language review, and training support, as agreed in writing.

2. Quotes and acceptance

Work starts only after written acceptance of a quote or proposal. Any change in scope may require a revised quote.

3. Delivery and client obligations

The client provides complete source materials and instructions. Deadlines depend on timely receipt of required documents and feedback.

4. Fees and payment

Fees are stated in EUR unless otherwise agreed. Invoices are payable within the stated terms. Late payment may pause ongoing work.

5. Intellectual property

Client source materials remain the client’s property. Service output is licensed to the client for intended business use after full payment, except where third-party rights apply.

6. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential information private and to use it only for the agreed services.

7. Liability

Services are delivered with reasonable professional care. Liability is limited to direct damages capped at the amount paid for the relevant service, except where mandatory law states otherwise.

8. Governing law

These terms are governed by French law. Parties will seek an amicable resolution before court action.
