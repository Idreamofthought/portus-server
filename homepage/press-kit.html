Portus

A concise kit for describing, sharing, and covering Portus, the mythic browser strategy game from I Dream of Thought.

Description

Short description.

Portus is a contemplative browser strategy game about building a fragile coastal settlement shaped by warnings, disasters, favour, and deep-time lore.

Long description.

In Portus, players gather resources, build carefully, interpret warnings, endure disasters, and uncover the memory of a world watched by Time, Chaos, Destiny, and Twilight. The project sits inside I Dream of Thought, a creative archive of writing, myth, philosophy, and interactive worlds.
