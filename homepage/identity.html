01 / Roots

Thought is not a straight line.

It rises, recedes, returns. It gathers traces: a damp path after rain, the smell of woodsmoke, a sentence remembered incorrectly, roots meeting in the dark.

I Dream of Thought is a home for those traces. It is part writing archive, part creative laboratory, and part invitation to pay closer attention.

02 / The writing

Language as weather.

The writing begins with the sensory world and moves inward. It is lyrical, seasonal, introspective, and sometimes quietly strange. Poems, prose, fragments, and essays follow the same question: what is moving underneath what we can see?

There are no tidy answers here. There are roots, tendrils, memory, breath, fire, leaves, and the feeling that a place may be thinking back.

03 / Portus

A game at the edge of the sea

What the writing imagines, Portus lets you inhabit.

Portus is a contemplative strategy game about building a fragile settlement, reading signs, and living with the forces that shape a place. Time, Chaos, Destiny, and Twilight are not just systems. They are pressures, moods, and ways of understanding the world.

The game is another branch from the same trunk: slower than conquest, stranger than management, and attentive to what a landscape remembers.
