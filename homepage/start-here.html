What is idreamofthought?

I Dream of Thought is a creative platform for poems, essays, dream fragments, worldbuilding, philosophy, and playable myth. It is arranged like a tree: branches for themes, leaves for works, and Portus as its flagship interactive world.

Follow a territory.

The tree gathers work by territory: poetry, personal prose, fiction, culture, science and nature, history and memory, philosophy, teaching, and the inner weather of health and psychology.
