What is idreamofthought?

I Dream of Thought is a creative platform for poems, essays, dream fragments, worldbuilding, philosophy, and playable myth. It is arranged like a tree: branches for themes, leaves for works, and Breluce as its flagship interactive world.

Follow a territory.

The tree gathers work by territory: poetry, personal prose, fiction, culture, science and nature, history and memory, philosophy, teaching, and the inner weather of health and psychology.

What is Breluce?

Breluce is a contemplative strategy game about building a settlement, interpreting warnings, and living with what the landscape remembers. Begin at the hub, then move through gameplay and the Codex.
