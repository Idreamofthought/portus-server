01 / Lore

A city built inside a warning.

You wake beside the sea with little more than a shoreline, a settlement to raise, and four powers moving through the harbour: Time, Chaos, Destiny, and Twilight.

The Codex records what the place remembers. Discoveries, disasters, rituals, and small acts of survival leave traces in the archive.

02 / The world

A port at the edge of the sea.

Portus is about time, fate, ecology, and the fragile balance of places. Gather what you need, build carefully, interpret warnings, and live with the traces your choices leave behind.

It is not a game of speed. It is a game of rhythm: a slow unfolding, like dusk settling over the Mediterranean.
