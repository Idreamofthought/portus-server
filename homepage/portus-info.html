Portus

Portus is a contemplative strategy game about time, fate, ecology, and the fragile balance of places. You build, interpret signs, survive disasters, and navigate the shifting favour of four forces: Time, Chaos, Destiny, and Twilight.

It is not a game of speed. It is a game of rhythm — a slow unfolding, like dusk settling over the Mediterranean. Every action has weight. Every warning has meaning. Every disaster leaves a trace.

The Four Forces

Time — the steady pulse of the world. It rewards patience and long vision.

Chaos — the unpredictable tide. It disrupts, reshapes, and tests resilience.

Destiny — the guiding thread. It offers rare moments of clarity and direction.

Twilight — the liminal force. Neither light nor dark, it marks thresholds and turning points.

Your Port

You begin with almost nothing — a small port, a few resources, and a world that watches. Build carefully. Interpret signs. Protect your people. The sea remembers everything.

Warnings & Disasters

The world speaks through omens. A warning is a whisper; a disaster is a lesson. Neither is random — each is shaped by your choices, your balance, and the four forces.

The Codex

As you play, you uncover fragments of lore — small texts, symbols, and echoes. The Codex grows with you, revealing the deeper story of Portus and the ecology of its world.
