Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Covers idreamofthought.org, including the Portus game and enquiries about CAST / RMJ English.

1. Who controls your data

Richard Jenkins, trading as I Dream of Thought (idreamofthought.org), is the data controller. Full legal identification is on the legal notice page. For any question about your data, contact richardjenkins@idreamofthought.org.

2. Data we collect

Portus accounts

  • Email address and a salted, hashed password (we never store your plain-text password).
  • Your gameplay/save state (captain name, city layout, progress).
  • Purchase records: amount, currency, product, provider, and date — payment card details are entered directly with Stripe or PayPal and never reach our servers.
  • Short-lived security tokens for email verification and password resets.
  • Technical logs (e.g. IP address, request timing) used for rate-limiting and abuse prevention.

Professional-services enquiries (CAST / RMJ English)

  • Whatever you include in an enquiry email — typically your name, email address, and message content.

3. Why we process it, and on what legal basis

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — creating your account, running the game, processing payments, responding to service enquiries.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — security logging, rate-limiting, and fraud prevention, balanced against your rights and kept to what's proportionate.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) — keeping accounting/invoicing records for the period French law requires.

4. Who else sees it

We use a small number of processors, each only for what they need to do their job:

  • Stripe and PayPal — payment processing. Each acts as an independent data controller for the payment details you give them directly.
  • Resend — sends account emails (verification, password reset).
  • Railway — hosts the application and database (see the legal notice for their address).

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area (notably in the United States). Where that happens, they do so under recognised safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and gameplay data: for as long as your account exists.
  • Verification and password-reset tokens: deleted automatically once expired (within 24 hours).
  • Short-lived payment-processing records (used only to prevent duplicate crediting): deleted after 365 days.
  • Purchase/invoice records: kept for the period required by French accounting and tax law (currently up to 10 years), even if you later delete your account, because we're legally required to keep them.

6. Cookies

We use two cookies, both strictly necessary for the site to function: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a CSRF-protection cookie that helps prevent forged requests. Neither is used for advertising or tracking, and under ePrivacy rules strictly-necessary cookies like these don't require a consent banner.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • give you access to the data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data (subject to the legal retention obligations in section 5);
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • provide your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these, email richardjenkins@idreamofthought.org. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or with the supervisory authority in your own EU country of residence.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text), traffic is encrypted in transit, and access to game/payment actions is rate-limited and protected against forged requests. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable, proportionate measures to protect your data.

9. Age requirement

Portus accounts require you to be at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.
