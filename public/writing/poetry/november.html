November

12 November 2026 — Poetry · Poems · Richard Jenkins

Short, cruel and cold,
dark wet winds bark.
Ice bites, leaves fold,
fly from trees bare, stark.

Hidden things speak, shine
through the short day's doors.
Deep Sleep for nature's vines,
Cold creeps, sweeps, pours.

Crows and magpies shriek,
and sinking hope flees.
Frozen words we cannot speak,
Frozen tears we cannot weep.
Even stones freeze, numbed, seized.

November sinks into our bones,
November sinks into our homes.
