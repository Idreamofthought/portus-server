Perspectives
Abandoning roads never meant
Taken to please
Rather than choice
Taken without purpose
Needed to pay the rent
Escape from tease
Wasn't my voice.



Minds not set or sent
Took the way of ease
Sentiments invoiced
Escaped from the circus
Mind set hell bent
Scraping my knees
Keeping my poise.
