Sandmartins Linger

2026-09-20 — Poetry · Poems — Tags: autumn, equinox, birds, nature


Sandmartins linger

Wisps sky painted

Sun dazed dazzled summer

Equinox

Flit dart dare

Wind caressed and carried

Tended by time

Windblown by chance

Sky tracks and traces

Slipping away 'till spring

Autumn's doorway ajar
