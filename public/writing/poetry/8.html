8

2016-04-08 — Poetry · NaPoWriMo — Tags: NaPoWriMo, nature

Yellow buds

Lilacs forsythia and daffodils flower at the same time

Even if this sounds strange

Warm winter

Buds yet to break

Maybe next time

Or never.

Eaten or broken

Yet the tree lives.
