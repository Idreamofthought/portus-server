2016-04-19 — Poetry · NaPoWriMo — Tags: NaPoWriMo, poetry

They write

Sit in their chairs and silently think

Ink goes on the pages and mistakes are made

Try to learn

But habits are harder to break than that.

Hoping for more

They scribble, pout, glance

Nibbling at their nails,

Arms posed on shoulders ,

Or across pages

Legs double crossed,

Head down held by elbowed hand

Work down in Modern letters or Art

Describing their ideas.
