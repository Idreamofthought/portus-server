7

2016-04-07 — Poetry · NaPoWriMo — Tags: NaPoWriMo, poetry

Stand.Hand out the sheets.

Talk listen ask tell show teach learn exchange

Build up.Paradigms.links between people not knowledge.

Hats feed brains screens feed brains facebook or lolcats.

Wired to knowledge without questioning its source.

Learn through the wire hat.

Seven. A day where we live.
