2016-04-10 — Poetry · NaPoWriMo — Tags: NaPoWriMo, birth, family

 

For Andy and Elise,  for Daniel.

 

 

Furled fingers grasp gently

Heardbeats heard in awe

Ploughing your love into new lives

In your arms new life

Pass on hope and joy

Larkin's misery melts

In those moments

There is no better

There is only Now.
