Earth day prompt

 

Make a change

Make it now

Make it you

Make it true

 

Chpping trees

Burning coal

Killing things

Watch them go

 

Far away

Out of sight

Living things

Dont get to fight

 

7 billion

Munching through

Turning cows

Into glue

 

Turning Whales

Into paint

Goodbye bees

Hello fate

 

We forget

The scales on wings

The jewelled beauty

Of living things

 

Packed lives

For pre packed fun

But hold your breath

How long be done?

 

So think and act

Dont chuck your trash

Dont use things

That cost less cash

 

If in the end

Those graced things die

And all because

Of you and I
