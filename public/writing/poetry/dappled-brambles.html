Dappled Brambles

2026-02-25 — Poetry · Poems — Tags: spring, nature, brambles


Bramble leaf-back, dew dappled

Damp morn, dawn,

Pearl lined spheres, sunlight shining,

Winter worn through, old socks stinking

Daffodils dancing, spring to life

Fireworks forsythia erupt, yellow swoon

Brambles tangles on stems

Buds ready to burst, flower or leaf?

First gasps green, Spring’s dream
