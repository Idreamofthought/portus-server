2008-09-05 — Personal · About the blog — Tags: blog, introduction, writing

This blog is designed for several purposes:

1) An outlet for my rage (only joking!).

2) A place to discuss various topics—hot or not—be it sport, climate, politics, knitting, and so on.

I hope you enjoy reading and that you take the time to respond. This blog will be written in French and/or English.

Bonjour.
