American nightmares

2026-06-26 — Politics & Society · Geopolitics & decline — Tags: USA, West, politics, democracy






If we are really honest, we’ve been watching the decline of the west for decades.



We, the west, sat on our laurels  and our world view was not that of informed people.



We learnt the world that existed the year our teachers were born.



I remember our teacher laughing in 1984 when we talked about super powers, when we suggested that China had the potential to become one. It had the population, but perhaps not the technology or the ambition. China was still a Communist country in 1984 and perhaps our teacher was right to laugh at us.



But since then China and a lot of Asia has come out of poverty. 1 billion Asians have been lifted out of poverty over the last 20 or so years.



Life expectancy has risen , technology has developed, medicine, education and the standard of living have all risen.



In China in 1978 very very few people were Doctoral candidates, now China has more doctors than most other places. They even introduced a doctorate if you produce a product, a practical PhD.



China and India seem to be outpacing the world. America is still the sprawling blob its always been, but we see the leaves turning colour, just as the British Empire post world war 1 and 2.



America has lost wars since Vietnam. You could even argue that the USA hasn’t won anything since WW2 and the emperors’ new clothes are invisible.



America went from a president who could not tell a lie, to one who could not tell the truth, and if I were to list all of Trumps alleged or even proven antics I would be here a very long time.



We saw the return of the coloseum to the white house gardens, with a fight for Trump's Birthday that left some participants bloodied and broken. Feed the crowd blood to keep them calm, it is as old as the Roman Empire.
