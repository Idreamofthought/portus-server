Orange Lizards

2025-06-09 — Politics & Society · Politics & power — Tags: political criticism, Shakespeare, USA


From William Shakespeare's Julius Caesar " Serpent's egg already hatched" to Dead Kennedy's " California Über Alles" people have often dared to criticise the poisonous aspects of power. " Power corrupts, and absolute power corrupts absolutely" Lord Acton said, and even The Bible in Ecclesiastes 5 8 onwards notes Solomon's observations that it is important for people to enjoy their work, but should others abuse that, no good will come.



I chose the burning image from Tolkien of Galadriel as I fear for America





Orange lizards, Basking
In the reflection of glory
Dropping names
Like a drunken fumbling
Gaslighting and catfishing
House built on sand just the same
Ego writhing in the light of lime
Ignorance of other's pains, problems
Slimy smugness personified,
Avatar of arrogance
Orders crimes and insists, heinously
Gross moral turpitude and turbulence
Breaking vows never meant
Dislike me at your peril
Criticise me and be deported, disappeared
I hate all, but I represent you.
My flaming face destroyed all
All will love me and despair.
