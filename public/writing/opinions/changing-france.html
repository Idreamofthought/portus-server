Changing France

2025-05-28 — Politics & Society · France & government — Tags: France, regions, administration




Proposal: From 101 Departments to ~60 Regions/Districts



Here’s a draft breakdown (examples, not an exhaustive list):



1. Greater Paris




  • Combine current departments of Paris, Hauts-de-Seine, Seine-Saint-Denis, and Val-de-Marne into one Metropolitan District.


  • Special governance structure, like Greater London.



2. Loire Bioregion




  • Merge parts of Pays de la Loire and Centre-Val de Loire along the Loire River.


  • Nantes and Tours as dual capitals.


  • Strong ecological and historical coherence.



3. Alpine Region




  • Merge Savoie, Haute-Savoie, Isère, and parts of Rhône-Alpes.


  • Based around the Alps and mountain economies.



4. Southwest Eco-Corridor




  • Combine Dordogne, Lot-et-Garonne, and neighboring rural departments.


  • Focus on agriculture, tourism, and ecological stewardship.



5. Occitanie Coastal Strip




  • Merge Herault, Aude, and Pyrénées-Orientales into a Mediterranean coastal region.



6. Brittany Unified




  • Bring all four historic Breton departments into a single Brittany region, as many Bretons desire.



7. Digital Corridor




  • A high-tech zone merging parts of Toulouse, Bordeaux, and Montpellier regions.



8. Post-Industrial Belt




  • Merge departments in the north-east affected by deindustrialization, with focus on regeneration.





🧠 Technologies to Consider




  • GIS Mapping Tools: We could create a visual, interactive version of this using QGIS or online mapping software.

  • Ecozone Overlays: Use European Environment Agency data to align administrative borders with ecological zones.





A prototype to consider







🌿 Prototype: Loire Bioregion (Département Loire-Vivante)



🔍 Concept:



A new administrative region that follows the Loire River, embracing its natural, economic, and cultural significance. It merges the central parts of Pays de la Loire and Centre-Val de Loire into a single bioregional unit.





📍 Included Areas (by current departments):




  • Maine-et-Loire (Angers, Saumur)


  • Loire-Atlantique (Nantes, Ancenis)


  • Indre-et-Loire (Tours, Chinon)


  • Portions of:

    • Loir-et-Cher (Blois)


    • Vendée (especially the northern Loire Valley areas)


    • Sarthe (Le Mans optional — could be part of a separate “Western Gateway” zone)







🌱 Why this makes sense:







🏛️ Governance Proposal:




  • Dual Capital Model:
    🏛️ Nantes (economic/transport hub)
    🏛️ Tours (historical/cultural/ecological hub)


  • Subprefectures:
    Angers, Blois, Saumur, Ancenis, Chinon





🚆 Transit Principle:



Replace the "horse travel radius" with:




  • Train/Car Transit Radius: Every citizen can reach a regional capital in under 1 hour by train or car.


  • Use existing TGV and TER lines to define natural hubs.
