# The World of Portus

Portus is a landscape shaped by deep-time forces: river shifts, stone memory,
ancient floods, and forgotten civilisations.

## Geography

- River valleys
- High cairns
- Forest dens
- Floodplains
- Drowned ruins
- Stone terraces

## Themes

- Deep-time continuity
- Myth as memory
- Landscape as archive
- Ritual as negotiation
- Disaster as teacher

## Civilisations

- The First Builders
- The River People
- The Beast-Clan
- The Drowned
- The Sky-Readers

## Tone

Mystical, archaeological, and symbolic.
