# World

## Overview
Portus is a suspended settlement drifting between dream, history, and memory. The world is shaped by movement, scarcity, and mythic atmosphere rather than only industrial expansion.

## Core setting ideas
- The city exists in a liminal spatial state, not fully fixed to any one geography
- Terrain, weather, and ritual practice all influence daily life
- Settlers are not purely practical; they are also steeped in memory and belief
- The settlement’s survival is tied to the harmony between infrastructure and story

## Thematic pillars
### Dream and memory
The world does not always obey ordinary logic. Time, memory, and ritual are active forces.

### Ecology and care
The city’s relationship with forests, soil, water, and weather is central to survival.

### Myth and labor
Work is not separate from belief. The culture builds meaning into resource gathering, place-making, and civic ritual.

### Drift and instability
The world is not static. Portus shifts, changes, and responds to the emotional and environmental state of its inhabitants.

## Geography
The world may include:
- plateaus and forested districts
- water-logged settlements or river edges
- shrines and ruin clusters
- thresholds between safe land and unstable dream terrain

## Design rule
The world should feel lived-in and emotionally legible. Even systems like production and disaster are part of a unified mythology.
