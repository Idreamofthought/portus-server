# The Codex of Portus

The Codex is the living record of discoveries, artifacts, disasters, myths,
and deep-time knowledge.

## Structure

- Artifacts
- Disasters
- Favour system
- World myths
- Drowned Civilisation
- Beast legends
- River prophecies
- Stone memory

## Unlocking

Codex entries can unlock through artifact discovery, disaster survival,
rituals, terrain exploration, quests, and favour thresholds. Runtime allowlists
and entry files must remain aligned with [`lore/README.md`](../lore/README.md).

## Tone

Entries may take the form of archaeological field notes, myth fragments, ritual
instructions, geomorphological observations, or cultural memory.
