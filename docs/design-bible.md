# Portus Design Bible

## Core Identity

Portus is a slow, atmospheric, deep-time exploration and city-building game.

## Pillars

- Discovery
- Ritual
- Landscape
- Memory
- Survival

## Player Experience

- Calm exploration
- Sudden disasters
- Meaningful rituals
- Gradual understanding
- Codex-driven storytelling

## Aesthetic

- Stone
- Water
- Bone
- Light
- Silence
