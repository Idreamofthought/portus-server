# Design Bible

## Core design statement
Portus is a contemplative city-building experience where survival, myth, and memory are inseparable. The player builds a settlement not purely for growth, but for meaning.

## Aesthetic direction
- dreamlike, atmospheric, and intimate
- soft contrast, layered translucence, and subtle motion
- quiet color palette with luminous highlights
- typography and UI that feel ceremonial or archival rather than sterile

## Mechanical direction
- systems should reinforce narrative and mood
- choices should be legible and emotionally resonant
- each system should be modular but interconnected

## Thematic grammar
The project should feel as though it was assembled from memory, ritual, and drift. Even function and economy should carry symbolic weight.

## Design checklist
- Is the feature evocative?
- Does it reinforce the world’s mood?
- Does it support the city’s emotional arc?
- Does it remain understandable without losing mystique?

## Summary
Portus is not about maximizing efficiency at all costs. It is about shaping a fragile, dream-anchored community that becomes richer through attention, care, and interpretation.
