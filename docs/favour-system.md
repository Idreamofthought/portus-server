# Favour System

The world responds to the player through five favour pillars:

1. Time
2. Chaos
3. Beast
4. River
5. Stone

## How Favour Works

- High favour can reduce or reshape disaster pressure.
- Low favour can increase omens and uncertainty.
- Rituals adjust favour.
- Artifacts can influence favour when their intended effects are implemented.
- Disasters shift favour as part of their aftermath.

## Example: Time Favour

Time favour is associated with Celestial Instruments and with observing
seasonal cycles. The category's intended effects are documented in
[The Seven Artifact Categories](ARTIFACT-CATEGORIES.md).
