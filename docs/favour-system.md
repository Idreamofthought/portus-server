# Favour System

## Overview
The Favour system represents the settlement’s relationship to unseen powers, inherited myths, and the deeper emotional logic of the world. It is not just a meter; it is a measure of resonance.

## Core meaning
Favour can rise or fall based on:
- ritual practice
- research choices
- settlement values
- response to disaster
- treatment of relics, ecology, or memory

## Why it matters
Favour influences the city’s ability to thrive, but it also changes the tone and narrative meaning of survival. It can signal whether the world supports the settlement or slowly withdraws from it.

## States and progression
### Low favour
The city feels neglected. Events become harsher, trust weakens, and the world appears indifferent.

### Steady favour
The settlement is in balance. Systems function and ritual becomes meaningful.

### High favour
The world responds positively: blessings, luck, unexpected support, and stronger symbolic cohesion.

## Design principles
- Keep the system readable and reactive
- Tie favour to meaningful choices, not grinding
- Use it as a narrative mirror for the player’s values
- Prevent it from becoming a hidden stat with no flavor

## Integration
Favour is likely to interact with:
- research unlocks
- city events
- disaster resistance
- codex and artifact meaning

## Final note
Favour is a way of making the world feel morally and emotionally responsive. It should speak to the player in poetic, not purely numeric, terms.
