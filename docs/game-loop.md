# Game Loop

## Core loop
1. Survey the land and evaluate resources
2. Place or improve structures that support settlement needs
3. Manage production, upkeep, and research priorities
4. Respond to disasters, warnings, and world events
5. Earn favour through ritual, cultural, or strategic choices
6. Expand the settlement while preserving coherence and narrative identity

## Rhythm
The loop should feel steady and reflective rather than frantic. It should alternate between planning, acting, and interpreting change.

## Design goals
- encourage small but meaningful choices
- surface tension between immediate survival and long-term identity
- allow the setting to evolve without requiring endless micromanagement
- create a sense of emotional continuity as the settlement changes

## Secondary loops
- research reveals deeper world knowledge
- codex entries deepen context and provide narrative rewards
- artifacts create long-term identity and strategic flavor
- disasters force adaptation and re-prioritization

## Result
The game loop should create a quiet but memorable cadence: build, observe, react, understand, and evolve.
