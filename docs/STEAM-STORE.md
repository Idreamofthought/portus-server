## Short description

Build a town by the sea in a world inspired by antiquity. Grow crops, connect workshops, care for your people and face floods and droughts. Discover buried fragments of the past as your settlement finds its own story. Play offline with local saves.

## About this game

A town begins with a few buildings. Its story grows with every choice.

Portus is a single-player settlement-building game inspired by the ancient world. Choose where to build, turn raw materials into useful goods, and balance the needs of a growing community.

### Build a working settlement

Place fields, woodcutters, quarries and workshops where the landscape supports them. Connect buildings with roads and develop production chains as your town grows.

### Care for more than production

Make room for community life, from gathering places to an outdoor theatre. Invest in services and flood defences while keeping an eye on resources and maintenance.

### Live with the landscape

Floods and droughts can change the fortunes of your settlement. Adapt your plans, research improvements and decide what your town needs next.

### Uncover its past

Discover archaeological fragments and read their entries in the Codex. Your settlement's milestones and consequences become part of its memory.

### Learn at your own pace

Start with the guided marsh lesson or explore freely. The Windows edition runs offline, requires no Portus account and keeps your saves on your computer.
