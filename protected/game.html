Welcome to Portus

  1. Start by placing a House and a Field on the grass.
  2. Watch your resources and population grow in the bar at the top.
  3. Use Research, Army, Goals, Taxes, and Save from the menu on the map.
  4. Roads boost nearby production by 15%; a continuous road to storage or a market raises it to 20%.
  5. Tap a House, Healer's Room, Doctor's House, or Gathering Circle to upgrade it in place.
  6. Survive disasters, grow your town, and discover what your settlement remembers.
