# The Long Measure

## Quest 1: Marks at Low Water

**Trigger:** Build an Observatory and experience one full day-night cycle.

**Objective:** Visit the shoreline at dawn, noon, dusk, and midnight. Record
one mark for each returning light.

**Reward:** `Reckoning Bone` artifact and the *Lore of Time* Codex entry.

## Quest 2: Seven Wanderers

**Trigger:** Own the Reckoning Bone.

**Objective:** Maintain seven active buildings from seven distinct categories
until the next dawn.

**Reward:** `Wanderers' Dial` artifact and 7 Favour.

## Quest 3: The Divisible Hour

**Trigger:** Own the Wanderers' Dial.

**Objective:** Store at least 60 of three basic resources at the same time.

**Reward:** `Sixtyfold Thread` artifact and a one-time research acceleration.

## Quest 4: A Measure That Bends

**Trigger:** Own the Sixtyfold Thread and receive a warning or disaster.

**Objective:** Resolve the event without losing a building, then restore the
three resource stores to 60 before the next cycle completes.

**Reward:** `Calendar of Returns` artifact, 12 Favour, and the title
*Keeper of the Long Measure*.

## Design Notes

The chain asks the player to observe, order, prepare, and recover. Its numbers
are symbolic rather than historical claims: seven for the visible wanderers,
sixty for divisibility, and a completed cycle for renewal.
