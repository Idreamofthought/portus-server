# CODEX: The Lore of Time

*Portus Codex - Cycles Chapter I*

## I. The First Breath of Time

Time did not begin with creation.
Creation began when Time **noticed itself**.

Before land, before rivers, before stones, Time was a **current without direction** - a drifting pulse in the dark.
When the First Breath came, Time divided into three movements:

- **The Flow** - what moves
- **The Memory** - what remains
- **The Return** - what repeats

These three movements shaped the world long before humans walked it.

## II. The Flow - The River That Carves the World

Time flows like a river.
It cuts valleys, shifts coastlines, drowns villages, exposes marshes, and rewrites maps.

The Flow governs:

- seasons
- storms
- migrations
- births
- deaths

It is the part of Time that **never stops**.

When the Flow weakens, the world becomes still.
When it strengthens, the world becomes wild.

**Signs of the Flow:**

- rivers changing course
- coastlines collapsing
- storms arriving early
- animals moving strangely

**In Portus:**
The Flow determines the rhythm of disasters.

## III. The Memory - The Stones That Endure

Time remembers through **stone**.

Every stone is a record:

- megaliths
- river markers
- terraces
- drowned foundations
- cave walls
- granite chambers

Stones hold the memory of:

- ancient peoples
- forgotten rituals
- lost languages
- vanished kings
- eclipses and comets
- the breathing of landscapes

The Memory is the part of Time that **does not move**.

**Signs of the Memory:**

- stones humming during Twilight
- roots glowing
- old paths reappearing
- ancestral rituals resurfacing

**In Portus:**
The Memory determines favour with the Three Forces.

## IV. The Return - The Cycle That Cannot Be Broken

Time returns in cycles:

- eclipses
- comets
- solstices
- river floods
- drought years
- stone alignments
- generational patterns

The Return is the part of Time that **reminds the world of itself**.

It is why:

- kings fall when the sky moves
- cultures collapse when dogma freezes
- landscapes breathe in slow rhythms
- rituals echo across centuries
- the worst of humanity hides in the shadows of caves
- the best of humanity rises again

**Signs of the Return:**

- two eclipses close together
- comets crossing old omens
- repeating disasters
- repeating prosperity
- repeating mistakes

**In Portus:**
The Return determines Twilight and Nightfall.

## V. The Three Forces of Time

Time expresses itself through three forces:

### 1. Time (Continuity)

The keeper of stability.
When strong: calm seasons, steady growth.
When weak: Twilight begins.

### 2. Chaos (Pressure)

The wild pulse beneath creation.
When strong: storms, fires, floods.
When weak: stagnation.

### 3. Destiny (Judgement)

The reflection of human choices.
When strong: prosperity.
When weak: blight, plague, misfortune.

Together, they form the **Cycle of the Stones**.

## VI. The Cycle of the Stones

This is the deepest truth:

> **We live, the living dying, in the cycle of the stones.**

The cycle is:

1. **Flow** - the river moves
2. **Memory** - the stone remains
3. **Return** - the sky repeats

Humans pass through the cycle.
Stones endure it.
Rivers carve it.
Eclipses mark it.
Comets announce it.
Kings fall to it.
Cultures forget it.
Oral history remembers it.

This is the cycle Portus is built upon.

## VII. The Oral World and the Elders of Time

Before writing, humans understood Time through:

- stories
- rituals
- landscapes
- stones
- ancestors
- seasons

Oral history preserved the truth that dogma later broke:

> Time moves.
> Culture adapts.
> Ritual breathes.
> Memory is shared.
> Truth is relational.

Baltic tree-speakers, barefoot hunters, Neanderthal elders - all lived in harmony with Time's movements.

Portus remembers them.

## VIII. Twilight - When Time Thins

Twilight begins when:

- the Flow slows
- the Memory dims
- the Return accelerates

Signs:

- colours fade
- roots glow
- storms whisper
- stones hum
- animals behave strangely

Twilight is not punishment.
It is **warning**.

If ignored, Twilight becomes **Nightfall** - the collapse of the cycle.

## IX. Nightfall - The End of the Cycle

Nightfall is when:

- Time breaks
- Chaos rises unchecked
- Destiny withdraws
- Memory fractures
- Flow stops

The world becomes still.
The stones go silent.
The river forgets its path.
The sky closes.

Portus must prevent Nightfall.
