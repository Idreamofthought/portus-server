# Whispering Mask

The mask is small enough for a child and has no eye holes. When held near a
fire, it repeats the last voice spoken in the room, but with one word missing.

The Beast-Clan used it during rites of naming. The missing word was left for
the listener to supply: ancestor, river, hunger, or home. No two surviving
fragments agree on the answer.

**Recovered lesson:** a name is partly what is spoken and partly what the
community refuses to forget.
