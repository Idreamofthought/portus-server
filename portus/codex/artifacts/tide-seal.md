# Tide Seal

The Tide Seal is a river-smoothed disk stamped with a mark that resembles both
a wave and an open hand. It belonged to no king. It was passed between the
people who maintained the flood channels, so that no household could close a
waterway for itself.

The mark appears on stones far inland, where the old river once ran. It is a
reminder that authority in Portus began as a duty to keep passage open.

**Remembered use:** settle claims over water, roads, and the work they require.
