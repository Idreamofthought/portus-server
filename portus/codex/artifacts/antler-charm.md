# Antler Charm

Two short antler tines are bound with river grass around a bead of black stone.
The binding is fresh whenever the charm is found, though the grass belongs to
no plant that grows near Portus.

The Beast-Clan wore these charms when entering a forest den. They did not claim
to command the animals. They wore the mark to announce that they knew the den
was already occupied by another kind of life.

**Remembered use:** ask permission before taking anything from a living place.
