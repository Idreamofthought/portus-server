# Star Reader

The Star Reader is a palm-sized slate pierced with seven uneven holes. It was
not made to measure distance. It was made to remember which lights returned,
and which ones did not.

The Sky-Readers carried these slates between the high cairns. Their marks turn
slightly each season, as if the stone is still trying to follow the heavens.
When held above a new settlement, the holes point toward the weather that has
not yet arrived.

**Remembered use:** read the turning of seasons and the approach of disasters.
