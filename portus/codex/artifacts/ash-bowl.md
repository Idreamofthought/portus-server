# Ash Bowl

This shallow bowl is black inside and warm even after a cold night. The ash in
it is not from one fire: cedar, reed, bone, and something sweet-smelling have
been burned together.

The River People placed an Ash Bowl at the edge of a gathering and spoke the
names of places they had lost. The rite was not a plea for protection. It was
a promise that the loss would be carried carefully.

**Remembered use:** make an offering before asking the land to endure another
season.
