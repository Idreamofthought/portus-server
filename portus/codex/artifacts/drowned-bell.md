# Drowned Bell

The Drowned Bell has no clapper. It rings only when lifted from water, and its
first note is always heard below the surface before it reaches the shore.

The Drowned used bells to mark safe channels through streets that had become
rivers. Their maps were not drawings but sequences of sounds: one low note for
depth, two for a wall, silence for a place that had disappeared.

**Remembered use:** listen for the shape of a drowned road before entering it.
