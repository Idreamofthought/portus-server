# Memory Urn

The Memory Urn is sealed with blue clay and packed with dry seeds. None will
sprout, yet they change shape when the seasons turn. A faint line inside the
neck marks the height of an ancient flood.

The Drowned kept such urns in their highest rooms. They stored grain in the
lower rooms and names in the upper ones, trusting that either might be needed
after the water receded.

**Remembered use:** preserve a future harvest and the story of the last one.
