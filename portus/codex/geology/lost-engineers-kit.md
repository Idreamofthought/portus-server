# Lost Engineer's Kit

The kit contains a plumb stone, a cord of copper wire, and three wedges cut to
different angles. Nothing in it is valuable by itself. Together, the pieces
show how the First Builders made a wall answer the slope beneath it.

The engineers left no plans. They left corrections: a shaved corner, a shifted
weight, a foundation widened where the ground remembered an older river.

**Recovered lesson:** good building begins by listening to pressure rather than
pretending the ground is level.
