# Shattered River Idol

The River Idol was broken by water moving in the wrong direction. Its face is
calm, but the back is carved with grasping hands and the names of seven banks
that no longer exist.

The River People did not worship the idol as a god. They placed it where a
channel split, asking the river to choose neither side forever. The fragments
still smell of wet clay after a flood.

**Recovered lesson:** a river can be a road, a border, or a warning, and it may
change roles before a settlement is ready.
