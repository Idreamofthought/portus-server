# First Navigator's Map

This map is drawn on thin hide with charcoal, salt, and a blue pigment that
fades in sunlight. It shows no coastlines. Instead, it marks where a traveller
could hear water, find high ground, or wait out a storm.

The First Navigators crossed the drowned country by memory and signal. Their
route ends at a blank inlet beside a single instruction: build where the land
can still be remembered.

**Recovered lesson:** the safest destination is not always dry ground. It is a
place where people can find one another again.
