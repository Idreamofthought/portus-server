# Spirit Atlas of Portus

Every terrain holds a presence. Spirits are not enemies to defeat; they answer
care, disturbance, repetition, and the choices made around their home.

## Landscape Spirits

### Mountain Spirit
Stoic, cold, and old enough to remember every fracture. It grants stability,
protection, and slow mineral wealth, but resents careless quarrying.

### Lake Spirit
A reflective keeper of memory. It offers clarity and prophecy clues; its deep
places yield Echoes-of-the-Deep relics, but only to patient fishers.

### Swamp Spirit
Rot, rebirth, and secrets gathered in one wet breath. It can bring sickness or
rare healing plants, and sometimes leaves a cursed object in exchange for care.

### Beach Spirit
A wanderer and tide-caller. Driftwood relics and sea lore arrive on its shore,
but its blessings are as unpredictable as the tide.

### Forest Spirit
Watcher, hunter, and whisperer. It reveals animal encounters and Beast-Marked
relics when woodland is protected rather than exhausted.

### Hill Spirit
Gentle, pastoral, and ancestral. It favors farms kept in balance and returns
pastoral artifacts to settlements that leave space for paths and grazing.

### Dune Spirit
Shifting, deceptive, and ancient. It conceals sand-buried artifacts, creates
mirage events, and remembers civilizations covered by the moving coast.

## Animal Spirits

### Bear
Strength, protection, and rage. Bear favors defensive resolve but punishes
wasteful hunting.

### Deer
Grace, fertility, and fragility. Deer improves harvest prospects while warning
that abundance can be easily broken.

### Rabbit
Luck, fear, and multiplication. Rabbit may multiply a small yield or scatter a
careless store.

### Whale
Depth, memory, and sorrow. Whale carries drowned-world clues to the coast.

### Bird
Omen, message, and prophecy. Bird calls announce weather, visitors, and turns
in the Return.

### Wolf
Loyalty, hunger, and leadership. Wolf strengthens coordinated labor but tests
a settlement that leaves anyone outside its protection.

### Lion
Authority, pride, and danger. Lion grants courage and status while tempting a
leader toward needless command.

### Horse
Travel, freedom, and storms. Horse improves trade and movement but can draw a
restless weather front.

### Snake
Wisdom, poison, and rebirth. Snake reveals hidden knowledge at a cost.

### Hedgehog
Defense, patience, and small blessings. Hedgehog protects a humble store and
rewards preparation.

### Badger
Persistence, mining, and stubbornness. Badger reveals a vein or damages a site
worked without rest.

### Toad
Alchemy, disease, and transformation. Toad turns a harmful condition into a
rare ingredient when treated with caution.

## Spirit Plants

### Moon-root
Gives a night blessing and strengthens Twilight observation.

### Ember-reed
Provides resistance to fire and heat-related disasters.

### Whisper-fern
Reveals a hidden path, lore thread, or nearby find.

### Blood-thistle
A powerful but dangerous ritual ingredient that may carry a cost.

### Cloud-moss
Influences local weather and helps prepare for storms.

## Geological Presences

### Rock Ghosts
Born from cliffs, boulders, and quarries. Rock Ghosts warn of collapse, reveal
mineral veins, or demand an offering. Their relics include stone masks, chisel
fragments, and echo-stones.

### Landscape Ghouls
Manifest when storms, quakes, floods, or droughts disturb the land. They may
curse crops, twist paths, or expose ruins. Their relics include scorched idols,
drowned charms, and broken boundary stones.
