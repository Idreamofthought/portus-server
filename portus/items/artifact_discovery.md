# Artifact and Archaeological Discovery

## Discovery Rolls

At the end of each turn, make one base discovery roll at **2%**. Activities
make one additional roll when completed: fishing **4%**, mining **5%**, farming
**3%**, and clay-pit work **4%**. A successful roll is 70% likely to yield an
artifact and 30% likely to yield an archaeological fragment. Only one discovery
may be awarded per activity completion.

Use rarity weights after a successful roll: Common 55%, Uncommon 28%, Rare
12%, Epic 4%, and Legendary 1%. A landscape or spirit can shift a relevant
rarity up one tier, never beyond Legendary.

## Seven Artifact Categories

| Category | Typical origin | Benefit | Codex thread |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Celestial Instruments | sky events, trade | reveal timers; Time favour | Sky, Cycles |
| Ritual Implements | temples, offerings | reduce disaster severity | Rituals, Warnings |
| Tools of the Ancients | mining, clay pits | lower build cost or time | Geology, Peoples |
| Tokens of Authority | trade, ruins | improve morale | Peoples, Philosophy |
| Mystic Containers | farming, ruins | preserve or store resources | Rituals, Cycles |
| Beast-Marked Relics | spirit encounters | improve related yields | Spirits |
| Echoes of the Deep | fishing, floods | water blessings and lore | Sky, Disasters |

## Archaeological Finds

Finds are multi-piece relics. A fragment is stored until its set is complete;
completion immediately unlocks its Codex entry and reward.

| Find | Fragments | Activities | Assembly reward |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Broken Calendar Stone | 5 | farming, clay pits, mining | predict one upcoming disaster |
| Shattered River Idol | 4 | fishing, river gathering | water blessing; reduced Chaos disaster severity |
| Lost Engineer's Kit | 6 | mining, stone cutting | advanced-structure research clue |
| Whispering Mask | 3 | rituals, temples, Beast-Marked finds | Destiny favour; hidden Codex entry |
| First Navigator's Map | 7 | coastal fishing, clay pits, deep finds | unlock the Raft Scenario |

## Artifact Origins

Each reward receives one origin tag: **creature-born**, **found**,
**disaster-born**, or **trade**. Origin changes its discovery source and lore
description, not its core category effect.
