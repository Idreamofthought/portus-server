# Spirit Encounter Events

Roll a terrain encounter only after an activity succeeds or a warning is
resolved. A settlement may resolve one spirit encounter per turn.

| Event | Trigger | Choice | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Humming Cairn | mining near mountain | offer stone or dig deeper | safety clue or Rock Ghost demand |
| Mirror at the Reedline | fishing at lake | wait or cast again | prophecy hint or Echoes-of-the-Deep find |
| The Rotting Crown | swamp gathering | cleanse or harvest | healing plant or cursed relic |
| The Tide's Gift | beach at dawn | keep or return driftwood | trade artifact or Beach Spirit favour |
| The Watcher Between Pines | protected forest | follow tracks or leave food | Beast-Marked relic or animal omen |
| The Old Grazing Path | successful farm turn | reopen path or fence it | Hill Spirit blessing or morale loss |
| Footprints on the Dune | dune after wind | excavate or mark the place | buried artifact or mirage warning |
| Seven Birds at Dusk | any Twilight turn | observe or dismiss | Weather clue or lost Favour |

Choices should reveal their cost before they are made. No encounter should
remove a building or permanently penalize the player without a warning.
