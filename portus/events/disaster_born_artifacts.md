# Disaster-Born Artifact System

After a resolved disaster, roll once for a relic: minor 10%, major 25%, and
catastrophic 45%. A relic appears only if the player stabilizes the affected
area before the event closes. This makes recovery, rather than disaster itself,
the source of discovery.

| Disaster | Possible relic | Immediate effect | Lore unlock |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Scorched Mask | temporary fire resistance | Ritual Implements |
| Flood | Drowned Charm | fishing yield bonus | Echoes of the Deep |
| Earthquake | Broken Boundary Stone | reveal a mineral vein | Rock Ghosts |
| Drought | Sun-Cracked Vessel | preserves food | Mystic Containers |
| Storm | Lightning Glass | reveals a warning timer | Celestial Instruments |
| Blight | Toad's Seal | converts one blight into an ingredient | Spirit Plants |

Disaster-born relics always carry a memory of their origin. Their Codex entry
should name the place, season, and disaster from which they were recovered.
